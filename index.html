BlogGEN

RAG-based blog generation app

OpsPilot

Autonomous AI task worker with human approval

  • A LangGraph plan-act-observe loop: the LLM picks one structured action at a time from 8 tools, while deterministic guards enforce step order and rules.
  • Drives a simulated accounting app through a real headless browser (Playwright), verifies the ledger result and detects duplicates to avoid double-posting.
  • High-value invoices pause for approve/reject, enforced in both the executor and the API, so the model cannot approve its own transaction. Includes retry-based recovery and multi-currency parsing with live rates.

Anomaly-Inspect

Unsupervised defect detection on MVTec AD

  • Compared a convolutional-autoencoder baseline against a PatchCore-style method (frozen ResNet-18 features + FAISS memory banks) across 15 categories.
  • The PatchCore-style method reached 0.977 mean image AUROC and 0.973 pixel AUROC, versus 0.669 and 0.505 for the autoencoder.
  • Exported to ONNX and deployed as a FastAPI + React demo with ~97ms median end-to-end latency on CPU.
